Highest-priority additions
1. Client brokerage accounts
Add a brokerage account record linked to contacts or companies:
- Account number, masked by default
- Cash or margin account
- Account status: pending, active, suspended, dormant, closed
- Account opening and approval dates
- Assigned trader/relationship manager
- Funding status
- External core-brokerage account ID
2. KYC/AML onboarding workflow
Add a dedicated workflow for:
- Identity verification
- Beneficial owners
- Authorized representatives
- Source of funds
- Purpose of account
- PEP/sanctions screening status
- Missing or expired documents
- Enhanced due diligence
- Review and approval history
This is especially important because securities brokers are AMLC-covered persons, and CDD includes customer identification, agent and beneficial-owner verification, purpose of relationship, and ongoing monitoring. AMLC covered-person guidance
3. Suitability and investor profile
Extend the existing risk profile with:
- Investment objective
- Investment horizon
- Trading experience
- Product knowledge
- Income and net worth range
- Liquidity needs
- Preferred securities
- Suitability review date
- Approver and approval status
4. Document vault
Support categorized, versioned documents:
- IDs
- Account-opening forms
- Corporate registration documents
- Tax forms
- Risk disclosures
- Signed agreements
- Proof of address
- Beneficial ownership documents
Add expiration dates, document review tasks, access logs, and download restrictions.
5. Read-only trading/account snapshot
Do not make the CRM the trading system. Integrate with the core brokerage or OMS and display:
- Holdings
- Available cash
- Recent transactions
- Orders and execution status
- Account value
- Last trade date
- Inactivity indicators
PSE describes the broker workflow as account opening, KYC, order submission, execution, and trade confirmation, so this information would fit naturally into the client profile. PSE investor workflow
6. Compliance and supervisory queue
Create a compliance page for:
- KYC renewals
- Suspicious activity alerts
- Unusual transactions
- Restricted or watch-listed clients
- Suitability exceptions
- Dormant accounts
- Complaints
- Supervisor approvals
- Manual overrides
7. Brokerage-specific pipeline stages
Update the pipeline to:
Prospect → KYC Pending → Documents Review → Account Approved → Funding Pending → Active Client → Dormant → Closed
8. Communication and order-instruction records
Add structured logs for:
- Client calls
- Emails
- Trade instructions
- Research recommendations
- Disclosures sent
- Consent and communication preferences
- Complaint records
These should be immutable or versioned and included in the audit trail.
Recommended MVP order
I would implement these first:
1. Brokerage accounts
2. KYC/AML case workflow
3. Suitability profile
4. Document vault
5. Compliance tasks and alerts
6. Read-only account/trade integration
7. Brokerage dashboard metrics
Keep order execution, settlement, custody, balances, and official trade records in the core brokerage/back-office system. The CRM should provide relationship context and workflow, not become the financial system of record.
For the Philippines, confirm retention, reporting, and approval requirements with your compliance officer and SEC/AMLC/PSE advisers; implementation requirements can differ by license and business model.
